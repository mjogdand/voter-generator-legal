Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Voter Generator ("the App", "we", "us") is published by Aigentools. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the App — and just as importantly, what we deliberately do not collect.

What we do NOT collect

The App's core function — reading voter-list PDFs and generating voter slips — runs entirely on your own device. Voter names, EPIC numbers, addresses, photographs, and any other data contained in the PDF or Excel files you process are never uploaded to our servers or any third party. All extraction, conversion, and slip generation happens locally on your computer.

What we do collect

To provide account access and wallet-based billing, we collect:

  • Account email address — used to sign you in (via Supabase Authentication) and to contact you about your account.
  • Wallet balance and transaction history — records of top-ups, job charges, and refunds, tied to your account, used for billing and support.
  • Payment details — handled entirely by Razorpay, our payment processor. We never see or store your card, UPI ID, or bank details; Razorpay passes us only a payment confirmation and reference ID.

How we use this information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session.
  • To calculate and deduct your wallet balance for jobs you run.
  • To automatically refund your wallet if a paid job fails to complete.
  • To respond to support requests.

Third-party processors

  • Supabase — hosts our authentication system and wallet database.
  • Razorpay — processes payments (UPI/cards) and is PCI-DSS compliant.

Data retention

We retain your account and wallet transaction records for as long as your account is active, and as required for accounting and tax record-keeping afterward. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Contact, below).

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing support@aigentools.in.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at, or intended for use by, children under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here and in the app, with an updated "last updated" date.
